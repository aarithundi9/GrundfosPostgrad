# Daksh — Week 3 Hourly Heat Demand Forecasting

I built an hourly heating-demand forecaster using a separate Ridge regression model for each of 17 building types. The target was aggregate hourly space-heating plus domestic hot-water consumption, and the inputs were weather and calendar variables only, with no previous heating-demand values. I compared seven linear/Ridge feature configurations, including heating degree-hours, piecewise temperature terms, and interactions with time of day and weekends, choosing each type's configuration using three held-out months within January–September 2018. I then refit on January–September and predicted October–December. My mean CV(RMSE) was **0.2509** versus the team baseline of **0.3918** (approximately **36% lower**), with improvement across all 17 types. Large hotels performed best (0.0792) and warehouses were hardest (0.8629), so the next step would be to inspect warehouse residuals and test generalization on a completely independent year. These results use simulated 2018 building loads and observed historical weather, not actual building meter readings.
